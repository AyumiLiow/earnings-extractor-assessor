First Internet Bancorp Reports Record Quarterly Revenue and
Continued Net Interest Margin Expansion
- Record quarterly revenue of $43.9 million, up 7% from the prior quarter -
- NIM expands 8 basis points to 2.47%; FTE NIM increases to 2.55%1 -
- Net charge-offs and delinquencies decline significantly from the prior quarter -
- Diluted earnings per share of $0.54; adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.571 -
- Company to hold earnings call today at 5pm ET -

Fishers, Indiana, October 29, 2026 – First Internet Bancorp (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: INBK), the parent company of First Internet Bank (the “Bank”), announced today financial and operational results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Key Business Updates

•Record Quarterly Revenue and Continued Margin Expansion: Total revenue rose 7% quarter-over-quarter to $43.9 million, driven by growth in both net interest income (up 7%) and noninterest income (up 6%). Fully-taxable equivalent (“FTE”) net interest margin expanded again in the third quarter to 2.55%1 (up 8 basis points). Additionally, when combined with well-managed expenses, pre-provision net revenue grew 14% quarter-over-quarter to $17.1 million1. Fintech partnerships revenue contributed to the growth in net interest income from embedded finance as well as noninterest income as fintech fee revenue was up 13% from the prior quarter.

•Overall Credit Quality Normalization on Track: Provision for credit losses for the third quarter of $11.6 million, down 13% from $13.4 million in the second quarter of 2026. The decrease was driven by lower net charge-offs, which declined to 0.82% of average loans from 1.77% in the second quarter of 2026, partially offset by specific reserves related to franchise finance loans placed on nonaccrual, including one large relationship the Company took action on late in the quarter ($2.6 million of specific reserves). Furthermore, total delinquencies, excluding nonperforming loans, declined for the second consecutive quarter and are down 38% from the prior quarter, representing 0.48% of total performing loans.

•Loan Portfolio Re-mix Toward Core Lending Areas: In line with the previously stated strategy, the loan portfolio continued to shift toward core verticals with higher new origination yields. As a result, the loan portfolio yield increased 8 basis points (“bps”) from the prior quarter. This reflected strong quarterly growth in investor commercial real estate, single tenant lease financing, wealth advisory lending and embedded finance, while previously exited, lower-yielding portfolios continued to run off. At the same time, the growing fintech deposit base allowed the Company to retire higher-cost CDs and brokered deposits, contributing to the margin expansion.

“Our third quarter results reflect continued execution of the strategy we have laid out for the last several quarters, with earnings momentum continuing to build,” said David Becker, Chairman and CEO of First Internet Bancorp. “Total revenue hit an all-time high in the quarter, driving positive operating leverage powered by record net interest income, continued net interest margin expansion and solid fee revenue growth. Additionally, credit trends continued to improve with meaningful declines in both net charge-offs and delinquencies. We also took proactive action on a larger franchise finance relationship this quarter; however, our underlying earnings strength allowed us to absorb the related provision impact and still produce strong quarterly growth in net income and earnings per share.

“The bottom line is that First Internet Bancorp is getting stronger every day. Loan pipelines in our scalable core lending areas are robust, and emerging verticals such as embedded finance and wealth advisory lending have exciting long-term growth potential. Our fintech platform continues to expand, driving growth in both fee revenue and lower-cost deposits. We also continue to invest in automation and AI-enabled tools across our lending and servicing operations, improving efficiency while elevating the experience we deliver for our customers and partners. Taken together, these trends create an exciting pathway towards accelerating earnings and stronger profitability. As we move into the fourth quarter and into 2027, we are confident in our ability to build on this momentum and deliver long-term value for our shareholders.”

About First Internet Bancorp
First Internet Bancorp is a bank holding company with assets of $5.9 billion as of September 30, 2026. The Company’s subsidiary, First Internet Bank, opened for business in 1999 as an industry pioneer in the branchless delivery of banking services. First Internet Bank provides consumer and small business deposits, commercial real estate and construction financing, SBA financing, public finance, consumer loans, and specialty finance services nationally, as well as commercial and industrial loans and treasury management services on a regional basis. First Internet Bancorp’s common stock trades on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol “INBK” and is a component of the Russell 2000® Index. Additional information about the Company is available at www.firstinternetbancorp.com and additional information about First Internet Bank, including its products and services, is available at www.firstib.com.
