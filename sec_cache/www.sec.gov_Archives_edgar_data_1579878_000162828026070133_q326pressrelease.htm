Figma Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Q3 revenue grew 43% year-over-year to $391.4 million, $16.4 million above the high-end of prior guidance.

San Francisco, CA – November 4, 2026 – Figma, Inc. (NYSE:FIG) announced financial results today for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“As code becomes a commodity, value is moving up the stack to design—and our Q3 results reflect that,” said Dylan Field, Figma’s CEO and co-founder. “We're building the full-stack canvas for visual-first development: one that's built for collaboration, increasingly powered by our first-party model and native to the way teams design and build with AI. This is Figma's opportunity and what we believe will fuel our next phase of growth.”

“Q3 revenue grew 43% year-over-year, coming in well ahead of our prior guidance as customers continued to expand seats and invest more deeply in Figma,” said Praveer Melwani, Figma’s CFO. “We’re seeing customers move from trying AI to making larger commitments, and the Figma agent is expanding how they use credits across our platform. That momentum, alongside improvements in AI cost efficiency, gives us confidence to raise our full-year revenue and non-GAAP operating income outlook while continuing to invest in our AI capabilities.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•Revenue was $391.4 million, up 43% year-over-year and above the range of Figma’s previously issued third quarter guidance.
•GAAP gross profit was $314.4 million, up 65% year-over-year; GAAP gross margin was 80%. Non-GAAP gross profit was $321.1 million, up 36% year-over-year; non-GAAP gross margin was 82%.
•GAAP loss from operations was $(119.2) million; GAAP operating margin was (30)%. Non-GAAP operating income was $47.0 million; non-GAAP operating margin was 12%.
•Net cash provided by operating activities was $48.4 million; operating cash flow margin was 12%. Free Cash Flow was $41.5 million; Free Cash Flow Margin was 11%.
•GAAP net loss was $(81.3) million and non-GAAP net income was $53.7 million.
•GAAP net loss per share, basic and diluted was $(0.15) and non-GAAP net income per share, basic and diluted was $0.10.
•Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $1.7 billion as of September 30, 2026.

Recent Business & Product Highlights:
•Net Dollar Retention Rate was 131% as of September 30, 2026.
•16,566 Paid Customers with more than $10,000 in ARR as of September 30, 2026, an increase of more than 3,650 since September 30, 2025.
•1,717 Paid Customers with more than $100,000 in ARR as of September 30, 2026, an increase of more than 450 since September 30, 2025.
•From July 1 through October 31, 2026, the number of weekly active users consuming credits among Paid Customers with more than $10,000 in ARR increased by almost 50%.

•As of October 31, 2026, on a weekly basis, 65% of Paid Customers with more than $10,000 in ARR were using the Figma agent and over 80% were consuming AI credits.
•During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Figma MCP server was Figma’s fastest-growing product as measured by growth in weekly active users. Total MCP usage grew 3x quarter-over-quarter, and write-back usage grew 4x quarter-over-quarter. In October 2026, Figma further improved and expanded its MCP server, launching integrations with 20 partners.
•Expanded the use of first-party and open-weight models in production. From July 1 through October 31, 2026, Figma more than doubled the share of work powered by our own post-trained design model.
•Began rolling out Code Layers, which brings working code to the Figma canvas, in open beta on October 14, 2026. With Code Layers, teams can build, explore, edit, and compare code-backed prototypes directly on the Figma Design canvas—and push them all the way to production.
•Hosted Config India in Bengaluru in October 2026, bringing together thousands of product builders and customers to launch new ways for teams to collaborate across design, code, and content on Figma’s platform.

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2026 Outlook:

Based on information as of today, Figma is providing the following guidance:
•Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook:
•Revenue between $402.0 million and $404.0 million, implying 33% year-over-year growth at the midpoint of the range.
•Full Year 2026 Outlook:
•Revenue between $1.497 billion and $1.499 billion, implying 42% year-over-year growth at the midpoint of the range and a raise of $33.0 million to Figma’s previously issued guidance.
•Non-GAAP operating income between $158.0 million and $163.0 million, representing a non-GAAP operating margin of 11% at the midpoint of the range.

Certain Definitions:
Figma calculates Annual Recurring Revenue (“ARR”) as the annualized value of Figma’s active customer agreements as of the measurement date, assuming any agreement that expires during the next twelve months following the measurement date is renewed on existing terms. A customer agreement is considered active when seats are provisioned to the customer at the start of their subscription. In cases where contracts are signed but not provisioned prior to the measurement date, the customer agreement is counted as active if provisioning takes place no more than 15 days after the measurement date.

Figma defines a Paid Customer as a customer account that is billed separately for which Figma has an active paid subscription as of the last day of the applicable period of measurement. A single organization with multiple divisions, segments, subsidiaries, or subscribing teams that are each billed
4



separately are counted as multiple Paid Customers. A customer account is considered active when seats are provisioned to the customer at the start of their subscription. In cases where contracts are signed but not provisioned as of the last date of the applicable period of measurement, the customer account is counted as active if provisioning takes place no more than 15 days after the last day of the applicable period of measurement.

Figma defines a Paid Customer with more than $10,000 in ARR as a Paid Customer with a total of $10,000 or more of ARR as of the last day of the applicable period of measurement.

Figma defines a Paid Customer with more than $100,000 in ARR as a Paid Customer with $100,000 or more of ARR as of the last day of the applicable period of measurement.

Figma calculates Net Dollar Retention Rate as of the applicable period of measurement by starting with the ARR of Paid Customers with more than $10,000 in ARR as of twelve months prior to such date of measurement (“Prior Period ARR”). Figma then calculates the ARR for those same customers as of the applicable period of measurement (“Current Period ARR”). Figma then divides Current Period ARR by Prior Period ARR to calculate Net Dollar Retention Rate for the applicable date of measurement. Figma’s Net Dollar Retention Rate reflects customer expansion, contraction, and customer churn. Figma calculates Net Dollar Retention Rate using ARR from Paid Customers with more than $10,000 in ARR because Figma believes that $10,000 in ARR is an important threshold, as it is a strong indicator of significant paid usage of Figma’s products.

Figma defines weekly active users as the number of unique users that access at least one of its products during a given week. Figma calculates quarterly growth in weekly active users using the week from each quarter with the highest number of active users.

Additional terms are defined in Figma’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed or to be filed with the SEC on November 4, 2026.
