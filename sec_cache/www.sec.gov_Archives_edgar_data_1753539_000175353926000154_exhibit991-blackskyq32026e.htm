BLACKSKY REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

Record Quarterly Revenue of $43 million, Up 118% YoY and 29% Sequentially
Net Income of $19 million and Record Adjusted EBITDA of $13 million
Company Raises Full-Year Guidance for Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA



HERNDON, VA – November 5, 2026 – BlackSky Technology Inc. (“BlackSky” or the “Company”) (NYSE: BKSY) announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Building on strong Gen-3 sales performance, we delivered an exceptional third quarter with record revenue, strong margin growth, and record adjusted EBITDA,” said Brian E. O’Toole, BlackSky CEO. “Real-time intelligence is more mission-critical than ever, and we are winning customers because of our demonstrated ability to deliver assured, actionable intelligence with speed, reliability, and clarity. As we scale our Gen-3 capabilities, we expect to continue our momentum capitalizing on the growing global demand for our space-based intelligence and sovereign solutions.”

Third Quarter Financial Highlights:
•Record total revenue of $43 million, up 118% from prior year
•Record space-based intelligence & AI services revenue of $33 million
•Cash balance of $231 million as of September 30, 2026

Recent Highlights
•Named the exclusive provider of very-high resolution optical satellites under a $1 billion investment through a consortium that includes France and the UAE for an initial constellation of 50 satellites to serve Europe and other markets
•Successfully completed a 2-month seven-figure assured Gen-3 pilot with the U.S. government
•International defense customer rapidly expands from a small Gen-2 pilot into a seven-figure Gen-3 Assured subscription contract in less than six months
•Successful Gen-3 pilot leads to seven-figure renewal contract with HEO to expand non-Earth imaging space domain awareness services
•Selected as the first satellite imagery provider and commenced a pilot with SkyFi’s satellite intelligence platform to power automated facility monitoring insights for Robinhood retail investors
•Successfully deployed a fifth Gen-3 satellite, achieving first light and revenue generating operations within 24 hours, expanding constellation capacity to support growing customer demand




Financial Results
Revenues
Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $42.8 million, compared to $19.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase of $23.2 million, or 118%, was primarily driven by accelerating customer demand for Gen-3, which drove record space-based intelligence and AI services revenue of $32.7 million.

Cost of Sales(1)
Total cost of sales as a percentage of revenue improved to 21% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 35% for the third quarter of 2025. The improvement reflects the operating leverage from the growth of the Company’s high-margin space-based intelligence services.

Operating Expenses
Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $33.4 million, which included $4.4 million of non-cash stock-based compensation expense and $7.5 million in depreciation and amortization expenses. Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2025 were $29.6 million, which included $3.5 million in non-cash stock-based compensation expense and $7.9 million in depreciation and amortization expenses. Excluding the non-cash stock-based compensation and depreciation and amortization expenses from both years, cash operating expenses(2) for the third quarter of 2026 were $21.5 million, compared to $18.2 million in the prior year quarter. The year-over-year increase of $3.3 million was primarily due to planned incremental investments in the business.

Net Income
Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $18.5 million, compared to a net loss of $15.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year improvement of $33.8 million was driven by strong operating results and changes in the gain/(loss) on derivatives, which are driven by fluctuations in the Company’s equity warrants and other equity instruments that are measured at fair value.

Adjusted EBITDA(2)
Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was a record $12.8 million, a 29.9% margin on $42.8 million in revenue. The year-over-year increase of $17.3 million was primarily driven by strong revenue growth, expanding gross margins, and operating leverage across the business.

Balance Sheet & Capital Expenditures
As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments totaled $231.1 million. Capital expenditures for the third quarter of 2026 were $15.4 million.

(1) Cost of sales is defined as space-based intelligence & AI services costs, excluding depreciation and amortization, mission solutions costs, excluding depreciation and amortization, and advanced technology programs costs, excluding depreciation and amortization.
(2) Non-GAAP financial measure. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below and reconciliation table at the end of this press release.



2026 Outlook
As a result of the strong third quarter performance, the Company is raising its full year 2026 outlook for revenue and adjusted EBITDA for the second time this year. The Company now expects revenue to be between $140 million and $150 million, and Adjusted EBITDA to be between $18 million and $24 million. The Company is maintaining its full year 2026 outlook for capital expenditures of between $50 million and $60 million.

BlackSky has not reconciled its non-GAAP financial outlook to the most directly comparable GAAP measures because certain reconciling items, such as stock-based compensation expenses, change in fair value of warrant liabilities, and depreciation and amortization are uncertain or out of BlackSky’s control and cannot be reasonably predicted. The actual amount of these expenses will have a significant impact on BlackSky’s future GAAP financial results. Accordingly, a reconciliation of BlackSky’s non-GAAP outlook to the most comparable GAAP measures is not available without unreasonable efforts.

About BlackSky
BlackSky is a real-time, space-based intelligence company that delivers on-demand, high-frequency imagery, analytics, and high-frequency monitoring of the most critical and strategic locations, economic assets, and events in the world. BlackSky owns and operates one of the industry’s most advanced, purpose-built commercial, real-time intelligence system that combines the power of the BlackSky Spectra® tasking and analytics software platform and our proprietary low earth orbit satellite constellation.

With BlackSky, customers can see, understand and anticipate changes for a decisive strategic advantage at the tactical edge, and act not just fast, but first. BlackSky is trusted by some of the most demanding U.S. and international government agencies, commercial businesses, and organizations around the world. BlackSky is headquartered in Herndon, VA, and is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange as BKSY. To learn more, visit www.blacksky.com and follow us on X (Twitter).

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income or loss attributable to BlackSky before interest income, interest expense, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, as well as significant non-cash and/or non-recurring expenses as our management believes these items are not as useful in evaluating the Company’s core operating performance. These items include, but are not limited to, unrealized gain or loss on certain warrants/


shares classified as derivative liabilities; loss on debt extinguishment; non-recurring transaction costs; litigation, settlements, and related costs; severance; and impairment, obsolescence, and asset disposals. Cash operating expenses is defined as operating expenses less stock-based compensation expense for selling, general, and administrative costs, and depreciation and amortization expense. The Company believes evaluating cash operating expenses is useful to manage expenses as it excludes non-cash items that may obscure the underlying business performance.

Adjusted EBITDA and cash operating expenses are non-GAAP financial performance measures. These measures should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to measures determined in accordance with GAAP. Please refer to the schedule herein and our filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) for a reconciliation of adjusted EBITDA to net loss, the most comparable measure reported in accordance with GAAP, and for a discussion of the presentation, comparability, and use of adjusted EBITDA. Please refer to the schedule herein for a reconciliation of cash operating expenses to operating expenses, the most comparable measure reported in accordance with GAAP, and this press release for a discussion of the use of cash operating expenses.
