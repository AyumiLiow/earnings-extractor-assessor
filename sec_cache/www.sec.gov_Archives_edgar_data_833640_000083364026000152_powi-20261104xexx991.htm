Power Integrations Reports Third-Quarter Financial Results
Revenue increased five percent sequentially to $125.5 million; GAAP earnings were $0.32 per diluted share; non-GAAP earnings were $0.46 per diluted share
Cash flow from operations was $42.6 million; quarterly dividend to increase to $0.22 per share in 2027

SAN JOSE, Calif. – November 4, 2026 – Power Integrations (NASDAQ: POWI) today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Revenue for the third quarter was $125.5 million, up five percent from the prior quarter and up six percent from the third quarter of 2025. GAAP net income for the third quarter was $18.3 million or $0.32 per diluted share compared to $0.17 per diluted share in the prior quarter and a net loss of $0.02 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025. Cash flow from operations for the third quarter was $42.6 million.
In addition to its GAAP results, the company provided certain measures not calculated according to GAAP. Non-GAAP results exclude stock-based compensation under ASC 718-10, amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, an accrual for a judgment in a legal matter, a restructuring charge recognized in the first quarter of 2026, and the tax effects of these items. Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $26.1 million or $0.46 per diluted share compared to $0.37 per diluted share in the prior quarter and $0.36 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025. A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial results and outlook is included with the tables accompanying this press release.
Power Integrations CEO Jen Lloyd commented: "Revenue grew five percent over the prior quarter driven by PowiGaN™ products as well as continued strength in the industrial market, where revenue is up 13 percent year-to-date. We also delivered strong earnings growth in the quarter reflecting our continued focus on expense discipline and profitability. Power is becoming a critical constraint thanks to AI and electrification, calling for dramatic advances in efficiency and power density. Our high-voltage technology and system expertise are well aligned with these challenges, and we are excited about our product roadmap and the opportunities ahead to drive shareholder value."
Power Integrations paid a dividend of $0.215 per share on September 30, 2026 to stockholders of record as of August 31, 2026. A dividend of $0.215 per share will be paid on December 31, 2026, to stockholders of record as of November 30, 2026. The company expects to pay quarterly dividends of $0.22 per share for each of the four quarters of 2027.


Financial Outlook
The company issued the following outlook for the fourth quarter of 2026:
•Revenue is expected to be in a range of $118.0 million to $126.0 million.
•GAAP gross margin is expected to be between 54.0 percent and 55.0 percent, and non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be between 54.5 percent and 55.5 percent.
•GAAP operating expenses are expected to be between $53.0 million and $54.0 million, and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be between $42.5 million and $43.5 million.
•GAAP operating margin is expected to be between 10.0 percent and 12.0 percent, and non-GAAP operating margin is expected to be between 19.0 percent and 21.0 percent.

About Power Integrations
Power Integrations, Inc. is a leading innovator in semiconductor technologies for high-voltage power conversion. The company’s products are key building blocks in the clean-power ecosystem, enabling the generation of renewable energy as well as the efficient transmission, conversion and consumption of power in applications ranging from milliwatts to megawatts in applications such as AI data centers, EVs and high-voltage DC infrastructure. For more information, please visit www.power.com.
