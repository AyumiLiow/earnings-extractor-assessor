MVB Financial Corp. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Company to Host a Conference Call and Webcast at 5:00 PM ET
(FAIRMONT, WV) October 29, 2026 – MVB Financial Corp. (NASDAQ: MVBF) (“MVB Financial,” “MVB” or the “Company”), the holding company for MVB Bank, Inc. (“MVB Bank”), today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026. The Fintech-enabled bank powering payments, banking-as-a-service and gaming programs for leading Fintech companies nationwide reported net income of $0.4 million, or $0.03 basic and $0.03 diluted income per share, for the third quarter of 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
Net income of $0.4 million, or $0.03 per diluted share, primarily reflecting a $9.9 million provision for credit losses.
Net interest income up 1.0% to $32.6 million, and up 18.1% year-to-date.
Core fully tax equivalent (“FTE”) net interest margin, a non-U.S. GAAP financial measure1, up 15 basis points to 4.02%.
Total cost of funds down 6 basis points, to 2.07%.
Fintech Payments deposits increased to $766.0 million, representing a 28.1% increase, from the prior quarter period and a 55.4% increase, from the prior year period.
Payment Card and Service Charge income of $5.2 million, down 12.8% due to seasonality, but up 39.6% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Onboarded four new fintech partners during the quarter, bringing YTD wins to nine new fintech partners.
Noninterest expense down 1.9%, to $29.8 million.

From Larry F. Mazza, President and Chief Executive Officer, MVB Financial:
“During the third quarter, we made tangible progress on several key initiatives that are strengthening our underlying business. Net interest income has increased 18% through the first nine months of the year, while core net interest margin expanded 15 basis points during the quarter to 4.02%. An important contributor to

Payment related deposits increased 28% during the quarter to $766 million, representing nearly 25% of total deposits, as we have now onboarded nine new fintech partnerships year-to-date. This growth is further diversifying our funding base and reducing reliance on higher cost funding, which is particularly valuable in a rising interest rate environment. “We also took decisive action during the quarter to address specific credit exposures, resulting in an elevated provision for credit losses that had a significant impact on earnings. THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS
•Net Interest Income, Net Interest Margin and Balance Sheet Trends
•FTE net interest income, a non-U.S. GAAP financial measure1, totaled $32.7 million, an increase of $0.3 million, or 1.1%, from the prior quarter, reflecting continued loan growth and a lower overall cost of funds. Net interest income increased during the third quarter despite a $2.3 million nonrecurring interest income benefit recognized in the prior quarter.
•FTE net interest margin, a non-U.S. GAAP financial measure1, was 4.02%, compared with 4.16% in the prior quarter, primarily reflecting a $2.3 million nonrecurring interest income benefit recognized in the second quarter. Core FTE net interest margin, a non-U.S. GAAP financial measure1, increased 15 basis points from 3.87% to 4.02% during the third quarter.
•Total loan balances increased $43.8 million, or 1.8%, to $2.52 billion at September 30, 2026, marking the sixth consecutive quarter of loan growth.

•Total deposits were $3.09 billion as of September 30, 2026, down $23.6 million, or 0.8%, from the prior quarter-end. Noninterest bearing deposits decreased $73.2 million and represented 32.3% of total deposits, compared with 34.4% as of June 30, 2026. Growth in payments-related deposits contributed to quarter-end balances, helping diversify the Company’s funding base during what has historically been a seasonally softer quarter for deposit growth, particularly in gaming and certain banking-as-a-service relationships. Fintech Payments deposits totaled $766.0 million as of September 30, 2026, representing 24.8% of total deposits, further diversifying the Company’s funding base alongside traditional banking, gaming and other Fintech relationships.
•The overall cost of funds declined by six basis points to 2.07%, reflecting progress in payments-related deposit growth and ongoing funding optimization, including reduced reliance on higher-cost funding sources.
•The loan to deposit ratio increased to 81.6% from 79.6%.

•Noninterest Income and Expense
•Total noninterest income declined $11.0 million, or 58.3%, to $7.8 million, compared to $18.8 million in the prior quarter, which included a $10.0 million pretax gain associated with a Fintech investment. Excluding that gain, noninterest income declined 10.5%, primarily due to seasonal declines in payment card and service charge income and lower equity method investment income, reflecting lower mortgage activity due to the increase in market interest rates.
•Payment card and service charge income declined 12.8%, to $5.2 million, primarily reflecting seasonal gaming activity, partially offset by growth in other payments-related initiatives. Income from this category increased 39.6% from the year-ago period. The Company continued to execute its Fintech banking platform strategy during the quarter through new client onboarding and continued progress across its payment business, contributing to growth in both payments-related deposits and payment card revenue.
•Total noninterest expense declined $0.6 million, or 1.9%, to $29.8 million, reflecting continued expense discipline, benefits from previously implemented cost-reduction initiatives and the use of AI and automation to enhance efficiencies.
•The Company continued to execute its Fintech banking platform strategy during the quarter and onboarded four new partners, bringing the total year-to-date to nine new partners compared to a total of five in the full year 2025.
•Asset Quality and Capital

About MVB Financial Corp.
MVB Financial Corp. (Nasdaq: MVBF) is an innovative bank powering Fintech solutions in payments, card issuance and online gaming programs for leading Fintech companies nationwide, while providing traditional retail and commercial banking services within established markets. MVB’s comprehensive platform includes money movement solutions across all modalities and embedded finance capabilities. MVB combines proven Fintech builder/incubator capabilities, innovative culture, regulatory expertise, core banking and AI-driven operational efficiency to enable Fintech partners to navigate complex regulatory requirements while accelerating time-to-market.
