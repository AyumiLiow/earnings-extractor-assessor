Streamex Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

240 net new ounces behind GLDY, up from 15 in Q2; Cask Foundation begins liquidity bootstrapping for USGY; debt-free with $36.5 million total liquidity position(1)

 

WINTER PARK, Fla., November 12, 2026 — Streamex Corp. (“Streamex” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: STEX), a technology company building the future of the commodity markets through tokenization, today announced financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

●GLDY growth: Gold behind GLDY rose to 3,351 ounces from 3,111, adding 240 net new ounces compared with 15 in the second quarter. The Company issued 208 new GLDY to subscribers, compared with 27.
  
●External ownership doubled: Outside holders owned 17.7% of outstanding GLDY at September 30, up from 9.0% at June 30. GLDY assets under management were $14.0 million at market value, up 12% in the quarter.
  
●Gold lease income: $145 thousand, and $291 thousand year-to-date. Excluding approximately $12 thousand of first-quarter income recognized in the second quarter, lease income grew approximately 8.2%.
  
●Operating expenses: $18.1 million, including $9.3 million of non-cash stock-based compensation. Of the $2.8 million increase, $2.2 million was non-cash stock-based compensation; the remainder was mainly external legal counsel and costs supporting product development.
  
●Net loss and Adjusted EBITDA(1): Net loss of $17.5 million, or $(0.09) per share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $14.6 million, or $(0.08) per share, for the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted EBITDA of $(7.2) million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $(6.6) million for the second quarter of 2026.
  
●Balance sheet: Debt-free, with a total liquidity position(1) of $36.5 million, total cash and investments(1) of $13.2 million and working capital of $22.0 million. Operating cash burn averaged $1.5 million a month, down from $1.6 million in the second quarter.

 

“Q1 launched GLDY, Q2 built the distribution around it, and Q3 was about scaling that distribution and adoption,” said Henry McPhie, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Streamex. “Over the last ninety days, an institutional investment manager began running a gold carry strategy with GLDY as its long leg, outside ownership doubled, and the Cask Foundation began bootstrapping liquidity for its first product, USGY. Next week, GLDY goes live as the first tokenized asset on Equity Trust’s IRA platform. This is not scale yet, but the drivers that can get us there are now live or under way.”

 

Business Highlights

 

●Equity Trust: GLDY is expected to go live next week on the IRA platform of Equity Trust, which has about $73 billion of assets under custody and administration and around 368,000 accounts. GLDY will be the first tokenized asset on the platform, allowing U.S. investors to hold yield-bearing gold in an IRA.

 

 

(1) Adjusted EBITDA, total cash and investments and total liquidity position are non-GAAP financial measures. See “Non-GAAP Financial Information” and the reconciliation tables at the end of this press release.

 

 
 

 

●Institutional allocation: An initial $1 million institutional allocation was made to a gold carry strategy run by an institutional investment manager, which holds GLDY as its long leg.
  
●Cask Foundation: Liquidity bootstrapping began in October for USGY, the Cask Gold Yield Vault, which holds its spot gold position in GLDY. The Cask Foundation expects to launch USGY publicly once initial bootstrapping is complete. GLDC is set up and ready, with launch by the Cask Foundation anticipated in early 2027. A Streamex subsidiary acts as servicer to the Cask Foundation and earns revenue from its products.
  
●GLDY Access Network: Several distribution partners with more than $160 billion of combined assets. CNB Custody went live in October, adding qualified custody for RIAs and advisors.
  
●Yield and attestation: Seven consecutive monthly distributions, every one paid in gold. GLDY paid 31 ounces in lease yield in the quarter, up from 19. Net asset value has tracked gold one-to-one since launch, and reserves are independently attested by EisnerAmper and viewable on Chainlink Proof of Reserve.
  
●New brand: Streamex introduced its new brand today, starting with streamex.com and its investor materials.

 

Commitments for the Next Ninety Days

 

Streamex will report on each of the following when it reports fourth quarter 2026 results:

 

●Grow institutional demand: more capital into the carry strategy, and new institutional buyers of GLDY.
  
●Support the Cask Foundation as it completes initial liquidity bootstrapping and launches USGY publicly, and report USGY’s contribution to GLDY assets under management.
  
●Fund the first GLDY IRA accounts on Equity Trust.
  
●GLDC: set up and ready, with launch by the Cask Foundation anticipated in early 2027 as USGY scales.
  
●Bring operating cash burn to a range of $1.1 million to $1.3 million a month, from $1.5 million in the third quarter.
  
●Continue independent reserve attestations and monthly distributions.

 

“We ended the quarter debt-free with $36.5 million of total liquidity,” said Christine Plummer, Chief Financial Officer of Streamex. “Most of our net loss is non-cash. Burn came in above what we expected in August, and bringing it down to a range of $1.1 million to $1.3 million a month while assets build is a priority. Our fees scale with assets against a largely fixed cost base, so once fees cover fixed costs, incremental assets should flow through to Adjusted EBITDA(1).”

Non-GAAP Financial Information

 

This earnings release includes information that does not conform to U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), including EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Total Cash and Investments and Total Liquidity Position. The Company believes these non-GAAP measures provide investors with useful insight into its operating performance and the financial resources available to support its tokenization platform, particularly given the significant non-cash charges in its results and the composition of its balance sheet, which includes physical gold, digital assets and other non-cash financial assets. Please refer to the reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures above.

 

The Company defines and uses these non-GAAP measures as follows:

 

EBITDA is defined as net loss, plus: interest expense (income), net, which includes non-cash amortization of debt discounts and issuance costs and imputed interest on the Company’s loan receivable; income tax expense (benefit); and depreciation and amortization, which consists principally of amortization of the intangible assets recognized in the May 2025 acquisition of Streamex Exchange Corporation. EBITDA is not adjusted for stock-based compensation or for other non-cash or non-recurring items, including gains and losses on the sale of gold, on marketable securities, on the extinguishment of convertible debentures and on changes in the fair value of derivative instruments, and these items are therefore included in EBITDA for the periods presented.

 

The Company uses EBITDA to evaluate its operating performance excluding the effects of its capital structure, income taxes and acquisition-related amortization, which management believes are not indicative of the cost of operating and scaling its platform. The Company believes EBITDA allows investors to assess its performance in a way that is consistent with management’s approach and facilitates comparisons with other companies that have different capital structures, tax positions or acquisition histories.

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA, further adjusted to exclude: stock-based compensation; the provision for credit losses on the Company’s loan receivable from Wintermute Trading Ltd.; changes in the fair value of derivative liabilities and embedded derivatives; realized gains on the sale of gold; realized gains and losses on, and changes in the fair value of, marketable securities; losses on the extinguishment of convertible debentures; gains on the settlement and forgiveness of accounts payable and accrued expenses; and other (income) expense, net. The Company uses Adjusted EBITDA to evaluate the cost of operating and scaling its platform excluding non-cash compensation and non-operating gains and losses, which management believes are not indicative of its ongoing operating performance, and believes Adjusted EBITDA facilitates period-to-period comparisons of its operating results.

 

Total Cash and Investments is defined as cash, marketable securities and digital assets (consisting principally of USDC, together with immaterial amounts of other crypto assets), each as presented on the Company’s condensed consolidated balance sheet.

 

Total Liquidity Position is defined as Total Cash and Investments, plus: physical gold held at cost (other assets held at cost – gold); the Company’s loan receivable, net, under its USDC loan agreement with Wintermute Trading Ltd., which the Company may recall on 30 days’ notice; and the Company’s investment in Metalayer Digital Fund I, measured at net asset value.

 

The Company uses Total Cash and Investments and Total Liquidity Position to monitor the financial resources available to fund its operations, platform development and growth initiatives, and believes these measures help investors evaluate the Company’s balance sheet in the same manner as management, as a substantial portion of the Company’s financial resources is held in forms other than cash.

 

Total Cash and Investments and Total Liquidity Position are not measures of cash available for immediate use and should not be used as indicators of the Company’s ability to fund its cash needs. In particular: physical gold is carried at cost and not marked to market, and the market value of the gold held by Streamex Ltd. was approximately $14.0 million at September 30, 2026, approximately $2.5 million below its carrying amount; that gold backs all outstanding GLDY tokens, including tokens held by third-party holders, which are presented as noncontrolling interests; the Metalayer investment’s net asset value may change and redemptions are subject to the terms of the fund documents; and recall of the Wintermute loan may affect secondary-market liquidity for GLDY. The amounts realized from any of these assets may differ materially from the amounts presented.

 

These non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for the most directly comparable GAAP measures, net loss and cash, as indicators of operating performance or of the Company’s liquidity or ability to fund its cash needs. In addition, these measures may not be comparable to similarly named measures presented by other companies.
