1 Pilgrim’s Pride Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results GREELEY, Colo., October 28, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) - Pilgrim’s Pride Corporation (NASDAQ: PPC), one of the world's leading food companies, reports its third quarter 2026 financial results. Third Quarter Highlights • Net Sales of $4.7 billion. • Consolidated GAAP Operating Income margin of 4.1%. • GAAP Net Income of $116.3 million and GAAP EPS of $0.49. Adjusted Net Income of $117.7 million, and Adjusted EPS of $0.49. • Adjusted EBITDA of $316.3 million, or a 6.8% margin, with Adjusted EBITDA margins of 6.9% in the U.S., 7.4% in Europe, and 4.9% in Mexico. • U.S. Fresh benefited from its differentiated product mix and improvements in live operations and yield efficiencies throughout the quarter to partially compensate for declines in commodity cutout values. Volumes remained steady compared to previous quarter given promotional support and increased distribution, offsetting the impact of planned downtime from the investment in Pilgrim’s Ellijay, Ga., plant to grow in the small bird debone segment. • U.S. Prepared Foods accelerated its marketplace momentum as volumes rose over 15% compared to last year. Just Bare® continues to lead growth in the fully cooked chicken category as retail sales grew by over 25% versus prior year. Construction of the new prepared foods facility in Walker County, Ga., remains on track with commission expected in the second half of 2027. • Europe sales and volumes remained stable as affordability of poultry and meal offerings continued to resonate in the marketplace, while inflationary pressures and impacts of excess pork imports from European countries continue to be a challenge. Approval process for the acquisition of Walkers Deli & Sausage continues to progress with an anticipated close in early 2027. • Mexico’s fundamentals in the live commodity market improved from previous quarters despite extensive availability of protein in the country. Sales grew as fresh branded offerings in retail increased by over 40% compared to last year. Growth and geographical diversification with ramp-up of operations in the Merida region and the Southern Peninsula remains on schedule. • Pilgrim’s published an update of its sustainability efforts through 2025 that detailed progress on its environmental, social, and governance ambitions. Since 2019, Pilgrim’s has reduced its global Scope 1 & 2 emissions intensity by over 20%. During the past year, the company continued to cultivate an environment for team member safety and well-being as Global Safety Index has improved over 60% compared to last year and more than 6 million training hours have been provided during 2025. • Maintained strong liquidity position to support future growth opportunities as the company’s net leverage ratio is currently 1.76x Adjusted EBITDA, below the target of 2x to 3x.

8 PILGRIM’S PRIDE CORPORATION Selected Financial Information (Unaudited) “EBITDA” is defined as the sum of net income plus interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. “Adjusted EBITDA” is calculated by adding to EBITDA certain items of expense and deducting from EBITDA certain items of income that we believe are not indicative of our ongoing operating performance consisting of: (1) foreign currency transaction losses, (2) costs related to litigation settlements, (3) restructuring activities losses, (4) asset impairment, and (5) net income (loss) attributable to noncontrolling interest. EBITDA is presented because it is used by management and we believe it is frequently used by securities analysts, investors and other interested parties, in addition to and not in lieu of results prepared in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the U.S. (“U.S. GAAP”), to compare the performance of companies. We believe investors would be interested in our Adjusted EBITDA because this is how our management analyzes EBITDA applicable to continuing operations. The Company also believes that Adjusted EBITDA, in combination with the Company’s financial results calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP, provides investors with additional perspective regarding the impact of certain significant items on EBITDA and facilitates a more direct comparison of its performance with its competitors. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are not measurements of financial performance under U.S. GAAP. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for an analysis of our results as reported under U.S. GAAP. In addition, other companies in our industry may calculate these measures differently limiting their usefulness as a comparative measure. Because of these limitations, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as an alternative to net income as indicators of our operating performance or any other measures of performance derived in accordance with U.S. GAAP. These limitations should be compensated for by relying primarily on our U.S. GAAP results and using EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA only on a supplemental basis.
