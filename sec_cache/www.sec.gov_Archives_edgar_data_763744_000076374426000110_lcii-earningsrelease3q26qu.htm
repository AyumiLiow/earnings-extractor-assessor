LCI INDUSTRIES REPORTS THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS
Growth in Adjacent Industries and Aftermarket; Strong Execution Drives Operating Margin Expansion

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights versus Third Quarter 2025
•Consolidated net sales of $992 million relative to $1,036 million
•Diversified businesses grew, with Adjacent Industries net sales growth of 7% and Aftermarket up 6%, partially mitigating lower RV OEM sales
•Operating profit margin expanded 30 bps to 7.6% from 7.3%
•Adjusted operating profit margin, which excludes the impacts of merger-related expenses and restructuring costs, expanded 100 bps to 8.3% from 7.3%
•Net income of $50 million, or 5.0% of net sales, relative to $62 million, or 6.0% of net sales
•Diluted earnings per share decreased 20% to $2.05 relative to $2.55
•Adjusted net income of $54 million; adjusted diluted EPS increased 12% to $2.21 from $1.97
•Adjusted EBITDA of $111 million, or 11.2% of net sales, up from $106 million, or 10.2% of net sales
•Towable RV content per unit up 6% to $5,733

Other Highlights
•Cash flows from operations of $316 million for the LTM ended September 30, 2026
•$28 million returned to shareholders via dividends during the quarter
•Strong liquidity position of $841 million, comprising $246 million of cash and cash equivalents and $595 million of availability on revolving credit facility at September 30, 2026
•As previously announced, on June 30, 2026, the Company entered into a definitive agreement to combine with Patrick Industries, Inc. in an all-stock merger. The Company will hold a special meeting of stockholders on November 18, 2026. Please visit www.PatrickandLippertTogether.com for more information.

Elkhart, Indiana - November 5, 2026 - LCI Industries (NYSE: LCII), a leading supplier of engineered components to the recreation and transportation markets, today reported third quarter 2026 results.

"Our third-quarter results reflect continued progress in improving profitability in a soft outdoor recreation market. Margin expansion and 6% growth in product content per unit contributed to our performance and position us to achieve our updated full-year outlook," said Johnny Sirpilla, Interim Chief Executive Officer. "Our 2026 performance has been driven by taking actions on those areas within our control. This includes the ongoing pursuit of operational efficiencies and strategic cost reductions. Our cost management and growth in adjacent industries and aftermarket sales contributed to our bottom line results and we believe leave us well-positioned as conditions normalize."

Mr. Sirpilla continued, "Our new product pipeline and the repair and replacement needs of the installed RV base provide opportunities to serve customers even as demand for new units remains constrained. We are also advancing toward the proposed merger with Patrick Industries, which we continue to expect to close in the first half of 2027. Until that time, our focus remains on disciplined execution and strengthening the business under current market conditions, while driving long-term shareholder value."

Third Quarter 2026 Results

Consolidated net sales of $992.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 compares to $1,036.5 million in the same period of 2025. The decrease in consolidated net sales was primarily driven by lower North American RV wholesale


shipments, partially offset by increases in selling prices contractually tied to indices of select commodities, growth in the automotive aftermarket, and sales from acquired businesses ($5.2 million in the third quarter).

Net income was $50.1 million, or $2.05 per diluted share, compared to $62.5 million, or $2.55 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income increased to $54.0 million, or $2.21 per diluted share, compared to $48.1 million, or $1.97 per diluted share. Adjusted EBITDA increased to $111.5 million, compared to $105.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Operating profit margin increased to 7.6% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 7.3% in the same period of 2025. Year-over-year margin expansion was driven primarily by cost improvement actions, including materials sourcing strategies, partially offset by lower absorption of fixed costs due to lower sales volumes and merger-related expenses.

*Additional information regarding adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted operating profit margin used throughout this release, as well as reconciliations of these non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, is provided in the "Supplementary Information - Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Measures" section below.

OEM Segment - Third Quarter Performance

OEM net sales decreased $57.7 million, or 7%, to $732.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $790.0 million in the same period of 2025. RV OEM net sales decreased 17% to $389.9 million, primarily due to a decrease in North American travel trailer and fifth-wheel shipments, and a change in RV sales mix toward more lower content single axle travel trailers, partially offset by increases in selling prices contractually tied to indices of select commodities. Adjacent Industries OEM net sales increased 7% year-over-year to $342.4 million, primarily driven by higher sales to North American transportation, marine, and manufactured housing OEMs.

Operating profit of the OEM Segment was $44.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, or 6.0% of net sales, compared to $43.6 million, or 5.5% of net sales, in the same period in 2025. Operating profit margin of the OEM Segment benefited primarily from increases in selling prices contractually tied to indices of select commodities, cost improvement actions, including materials sourcing strategies, and a favorable shift in component sales mix, partially offset by the impact of fixed costs spread over decreased production volumes, decreases in selling prices for certain products primarily related to changes in tariff costs, higher material and freight costs, including higher steel and aluminum costs and rising fuel costs, as well as merger-related expenses.

Aftermarket Segment - Third Quarter Performance

Aftermarket net sales increased 6% to $260.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $246.5 million in the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by increases in volume in the automotive aftermarket.

Operating profit of the Aftermarket Segment was $30.9 million, or 11.9% of net sales in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $31.9 million, or 12.9% of net sales, in the same period of 2025. Operating profit margin of the Aftermarket Segment was negatively impacted by higher material and freight costs related to tariffs, higher steel and aluminum costs, rising fuel costs, investments in capacity and distribution to support the Aftermarket Segment, restructuring costs to support growth in the automotive aftermarket, and merger-related expenses, partially offset by the impact of fixed costs spread over increased automotive aftermarket production volumes, cost improvement actions, including materials sourcing strategies, and increases in selling prices for targeted products and to cover increased material costs.

Income Taxes

The Company's effective tax rate was 26.7% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to 25.6% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily due to the reduction in a


discrete tax benefit related to an increase in the cash surrender value of company-owned life insurance policies compared to the prior year period and a write-off of projected non-deductible deferred executive compensation.

Balance Sheet and Other Items

At September 30, 2026, the Company's cash and cash equivalents balance was $245.6 million, relative to $222.6 million at December 31, 2025. The Company used $92.0 million to pay off the remaining balance of its 2026 Convertible Notes at maturity, $83.8 million for dividend payments to shareholders, and $41.9 million for capital expenditures in the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

The Company's outstanding long-term indebtedness, including current maturities, was $852.4 million at September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $595.4 million of borrowing availability under its revolving credit facility.

Outlook

Based on current market and economic conditions along with existing tariffs, the Company currently expects the following:

•October 2026 net sales of approximately $341 million, down 10% from prior year
•2026 North American RV wholesale shipments of 285,000 to 295,000
•2026 revenue of $3.9 billion to $4.0 billion
•2026 adjusted operating profit margin of 7.5% to 8.0%
•2026 adjusted EPS of $8.25 to $8.50

In addition to reporting financial results in accordance with U.S. GAAP, the Company has provided the non-GAAP performance measures of Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net sales, adjusted operating profit margin, Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of adjusted net sales, adjusted net income, and Adjusted EPS to illustrate and improve comparability of its results from period to period. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income before interest expense, net, provision for income taxes, depreciation expense, amortization expense, loss on extinguishment of debt, gain on sale of real estate, restructuring costs, merger expenses, the net impact of IEEPA tariff refunds, and executive separation costs, as applicable, during the three and nine month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. Adjusted net sales is defined as net sales adjusted for the reduction in net sales related to IEEPA tariff refunds passed through or expected to be passed through to customers, during the nine month period ended September 30, 2026. Adjusted operating profit margin is defined as operating profit margin adjusted for restructuring costs, merger expenses, the net impact of IEEPA tariff refunds, and executive separation costs, as applicable, during the three and nine month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. Adjusted net income is defined as net income adjusted for loss on extinguishment of debt, gain on sale of real estate, restructuring costs, merger expenses, the net impact of IEEPA tariff refunds, including interest income, executive separation costs, and the related tax effects, as applicable, during the three and nine month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. Adjusted EPS is defined as adjusted net income divided by weighted average common shares outstanding - diluted. The restructuring costs adjusted out of the non-GAAP measures relate to the Company's plant consolidations at our U.S. glass and automotive aftermarket facilities. The Company considers these non-GAAP measures in evaluating and managing the Company's operations and believes that discussion of results adjusted for these items is meaningful to investors because it provides a useful analysis of ongoing underlying operating trends. These measures are not in accordance with, nor are they substitutes for, GAAP measures, and they may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.
