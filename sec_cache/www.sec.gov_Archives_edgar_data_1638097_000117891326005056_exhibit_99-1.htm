Entera Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Updates

 

Clinical Preparations Advanced for EB613 Phase 3 Osteoporosis Study, with First Site Activation Expected in Late 2026

 

Clinical and Commercial Leadership Expanded with Appointment of Senior Executives and Directors

 

EB613 Enhanced Anabolic Effect Highlighted at ASBMR Plenary Poster Presentation

 

IND-Enabling Activities Ongoing for EB612, in Development with OPKO for Hypoparathyroidism

 

TEL AVIV, Israel, November 6, 2026 -- Entera Bio Ltd. (NASDAQ: ENTX) (“Entera” or the “Company”), a leader in the development of oral peptides, today reported financial results and key business updates for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“The third quarter of 2026 transitioned our expanding team into late-stage clinical execution mode. The opportunity to evaluate the safety and efficacy of EB613 as the first oral anabolic treatment for osteoporosis has generated excitement among clinical investigators and patients. Our team is fully focused on ensuring a seamless trial launch, with expected first site activation in late 2026. Additionally, the post-hoc analysis that we presented at ASBMR 2026 demonstrated that our Phase 2 6-month clinical data with EB613 show rapid stimulation of bone formation and robust early bone mineral density (BMD) accrual, especially in patients who start with lower BMD and who need the largest gains. These are the women we plan to enroll in our Phase 3 study. Finally, we continue to invest in expanding our human capital at Entera with early and late-stage clinical leadership and board appointments,” said Miranda Toledano, Chief Executive Officer of Entera.

EB613: First Oral PTH(1-34) Anabolic Tablet for Osteoporosis

 

•New Phase 2 Analysis Presented as Plenary Poster at ASBMR 2026: On October 9, 2026, Entera presented new Phase 2 post-hoc analysis of EB613 in postmenopausal women with osteoporosis at high risk of fracture in a plenary poster presentation at the American Society for Bone and Mineral Research (ASBMR) 2026 Annual Meeting. Changes in bone turnover markers (increase in bone formation and suppression of bone resorption) were enhanced in women with T-scores of -2.5 or lower. This post-hoc analysis supports the potential benefit of EB613 within the population that is intended to be enrolled in Entera’s Phase 3 study.

 

•Phase 3 Study Design Presented at ASBMR 2026: In October 2026, Entera presented the design of its planned Phase 3 study of EB613 at the ASBMR 2026 Annual Meeting. The study is expected to enroll approximately 900 postmenopausal women with osteoporosis, randomized 2:1 to EB613 (N ~ 600) or placebo (N ~ 300). Eligible participants will include postmenopausal women 50-90 years of age and ≥5 years from the last menstrual period with osteoporosis diagnosed by DXA with BMD T-score ≤-2.5 at either lumbar spine, total hip, or femoral neck. Participants completing 12 months of treatment in the registrational study will be eligible to enroll in the planned extension study through Month 24.

 

•Phase 3 Initiation Planned for Late 2026: In June 2026, Entera announced that the FDA accepted its proposed registrational development plan for EB613 and that a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial with a primary endpoint of percent change from baseline in total hip BMD at Month 12 may support the NDA. In addition, under the 505(b)(2) regulatory pathway, the NDA package will include a scientific bridging analysis with Forteo® (teriparatide SC injection, Eli Lilly) with separate population PK and PK substudies planned. Entera also intends to conduct a transiliac crest bone biopsy substudy in a small subset of patients as part of the registrational plan. Entera is preparing to activate the first clinical site for the Phase 3 study in late 2026, with topline results anticipated in the second half of 2028.

 

EB612: First-in-Class Oral Acylated PTH(1-34) Replacement Tablet for Hypoparathyroidism

 

•Phase 1 Initiation Planned for 2027: Entera is conducting IND-enabling studies for EB612 in collaboration with OPKO Health (“OPKO”) and plans to initiate a Phase 1 single ascending dose (SAD) study in 2027.

 

Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

Cash and cash equivalents were $265.9 million as of September 30, 2026, and restricted cash was $6.5 million, primarily designated to fund the OPKO collaboration. In July 2026, Entera announced that it had completed an oversubscribed private placement with gross proceeds of approximately $275.0 million before deducting placement agent fees and offering expenses. The Private Placement was led by existing investor BVF Partners L.P. (“BVF”), with participation from new investors, including Longitude Capital, Vivo Capital, TCGX, Spruce Street Capital, Venrock Healthcare Capital Partners, RA Capital Management, Perceptive Advisors, Driehaus Capital Management, Logos Capital, and Catalio Capital Management, among others. The Company’s cash resources are expected to be sufficient to fully fund the planned Phase 3 registrational program of EB613 through topline results and the anticipated submission of an NDA to the FDA and to support the Company’s operations into 2030.

 

Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $4.9 million, as compared to $1.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase of $3.3 million was primarily attributable to start-up activities for the EB613 Phase 3 program and to activities under the OPKO collaboration.

 

General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $2.3 million, as compared to $1.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

 

 

Net loss was $15.6 million, or $0.11 per ordinary share (basic and diluted), for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $3.2 million, or $0.07 per ordinary share (basic and diluted), for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily driven by a $7.5 million non-cash fair value remeasurement of the pre-funded warrants issued in our April 2026 and July 2026 private placements, which were reclassified to equity in August 2026.

 

About Entera

 

Entera is a clinical stage company focused on developing oral peptide and protein replacement therapies for significant unmet medical needs where an oral tablet form holds the potential to transform the standard of care. The Company leverages a disruptive and proprietary technology platform (N-Tab®) and its pipeline of first-in-class oral peptide programs. The Company’s most advanced product candidate, EB613 (oral PTH(1-34)), is being developed as the first oral, osteoanabolic (bone building) once-daily tablet for osteoporosis. A placebo-controlled, dose-ranging Phase 2 study of EB613 tablets (n = 161) met primary (PD/bone turnover biomarker) and secondary endpoints (BMD). Entera is also developing the first oral acylated PTH(1-34) tablet as a replacement therapy for patients with hypoparathyroidism (EB612), the first oral oxyntomodulin, a dual targeted GLP1/glucagon peptide tablet for the treatment of obesity and metabolic syndromes; and the first oral GLP-2 tablet as an injection-free alternative for patients suffering from rare malabsorption conditions such as short bowel syndrome in collaboration with OPKO Health, Inc. For more information on Entera, visit www.enterabio.com or follow us on LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Facebook.
