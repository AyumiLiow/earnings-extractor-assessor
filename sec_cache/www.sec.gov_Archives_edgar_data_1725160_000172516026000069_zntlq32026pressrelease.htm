Zentalis Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Updates
•DENALI Part 1b survival data strengthen azenosertib's position as the potential first-in-class oral, non-chemotherapy, non-ADC therapy in pivotal development for Cyclin E1-positive PROC
•DENALI Part 2c enrollment scheduled to complete in November 2026, with an integrated topline readout expected in 1H 2027
•$228.9 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026 provides runway into 1H 2028 to support execution of key milestones

SAN DIEGO, November 9, 2026 — Zentalis® Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: ZNTL), a clinical oncology innovator advancing late-stage development of an investigational, potentially first-in-class WEE1 inhibitor, azenosertib, as a biomarker-driven treatment approach for ovarian cancer, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and highlighted recent business updates.

"Overall survival data from DENALI Part 1b presented at ESMO reinforce our confidence in azenosertib to offer a differentiated oral treatment for patients with Cyclin E1-positive platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (PROC), if approved," said Julie Eastland, Chief Executive Officer of Zentalis Pharmaceuticals. "We designed complementary paths through DENALI and ASPENOVA to support our goal of bringing azenosertib to patients as quickly as possible. In light of an evolving landscape in PROC, we plan to discuss the DENALI data with the FDA in 2027 to assess the accelerated approval opportunity. Meanwhile, we continue to advance ASPENOVA at full speed, ensuring we are well positioned to support our regulatory strategy under either an accelerated or full approval pathway. With DENALI Part 2c enrollment completing this month, we expect a topline readout from DENALI in the first half of 2027 — a key potential value-creating catalyst ahead of ASPENOVA.

Ms. Eastland continued: "Alongside our PROC program, our strategy includes advancing azenosertib as a combination therapy where we see meaningful therapeutic and scientific rationale. MUIR, evaluating azenosertib in combination with bevacizumab in platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer, is enrolling. Additionally, we are actively planning a combination clinical study in triple-negative breast cancer. Collectively, this strategy positions azenosertib to realize its full potential, both as the first targeted option for patients with Cyclin E1-positive PROC and through combinations across tumor types where WEE1 inhibition may enhance the benefit of standard-of-care therapies, including ADCs and other agents.

“With $228.9 million on the balance sheet providing an anticipated runway into the first half of 2028, we believe we are well resourced to support key DENALI milestones, full enrollment in ASPENOVA and combination trials in ovarian cancer and triple-negative breast cancer,” Ms. Eastland concluded.




Business Updates
Clinical Development
•DENALI Part 2 Enrollment Completing: Enrollment in DENALI Parts 2a and 2b is complete and enrollment in Part 2c is scheduled to complete in November 2026.
•DENALI Part 2 Topline Readout: Zentalis expects to provide a topline readout in the first half of 2027 which will reflect an integrated dataset across Parts 2a, 2b, and 2c.
•ASPENOVA Phase 3 Enrolling: We continue to execute on the global Phase 3 ASPENOVA study, with trial site initiations and enrollment advancing across multiple geographies.
•MUIR Phase 1b Enrolling: Enrollment in Part 2 is ongoing for azenosertib in combination with bevacizumab as the maintenance treatment for second-line platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer.
•Triple-Negative Breast Cancer Combination Study Planning: Zentalis is actively planning a combination clinical study of azenosertib in triple-negative breast cancer.

Congresses and Publications
•European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) 2026: In October 2026, the following were presented:
◦DENALI Part 1b Overall Survival Analysis: Meaningful overall survival data from DENALI Part 1b were presented at the ESMO Annual Meeting, further supporting the biomarker-driven development strategy. In Cyclin E1-positive PROC patients (n=48), median OS was 15.9 months (95% CI, 8.7–21.2), versus 7.5 months (95% CI, 5.8-12.3) in Cyclin E1-negative patients (n=46). Updated ORR in response-evaluable Cyclin E1-positive patients was 37.2%, with a median DOR of 5.5 months, including two patients with responses exceeding 15 and 20 months. The safety profile was consistent with prior reporting; median relative dose intensity was 84.6%.
◦ASPENOVA Trial-in-Progress: An overview of the ongoing Phase 3 ASPENOVA trial evaluating azenosertib in patients with Cyclin E1-positive PROC.
•College of American Pathologists Annual Meeting — Cyclin E1 IHC Assay Validation: In October 2026, Zentalis scientists presented analytical validation data for a laboratory-developed IHC test for Cyclin E1 detection in high-grade serous ovarian cancer, demonstrating high concordance across laboratories and readers and supporting prospective biomarker screening in ongoing clinical trials.

equivalents and marketable securities will be sufficient to fund operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into the first half of 2028.

The $0.7 million decrease was primarily attributable to a $6.2 million decrease in clinical trial expenses for certain studies that reached or are near completion, partially offset by a $3.8 million increase in expenses for ongoing clinical trials open for enrollment, a $0.8 million increase in companion diagnostic development expenses and a $0.9 million increase in personnel-related expenses and allocated overhead.
•General and Administrative Expenses: General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $8.8 million, compared to $10.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The $2.0 million decrease was primarily attributable to a $2.7 million decrease in personnel-related expenses partially offset by a $0.7 million increase primarily related to consulting and outside services.
•Total Operating Expenses: Total operating expenses were $31.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $33.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

About Azenosertib 
Azenosertib is an investigational, potentially first-in-class, selective, and orally bioavailable inhibitor of WEE1 currently being evaluated in clinical studies in ovarian cancer and additional tumor types. WEE1 acts as a master regulator of the G1-S and G2-M cell cycle checkpoints, through negative regulation of both CDK1 and CDK2, to prevent replication of cells with damaged DNA. By inhibiting WEE1, azenosertib enables cell cycle progression, despite high levels of DNA damage, thereby resulting in the accumulation of DNA damage and leading to mitotic catastrophe and cancer cell death.

Azenosertib is in late-stage development as a potential treatment for Cyclin E1-positive PROC. There is currently no approved treatment option specifically for this biomarker-selected population which comprises approximately 50% of PROC patients. Cyclin E1 protein overexpression has been established as a sensitive and specific predictive biomarker for identifying patients who could potentially derive benefit from azenosertib treatment.

Azenosertib has not been approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or any other regulatory authority.

About DENALI Clinical Trial 
DENALI is a multi-part Phase 2 clinical trial (NCT05128825) studying azenosertib in PROC patients.

Part 1b enrolled patients with PROC regardless of Cyclin E1 protein expression, all treated at 400mg QD 5:2. Part 2 is prospectively enrolling PROC patients with Cyclin E1 protein overexpression based on Zentalis' proprietary immunohistochemistry cutoff.




Part 2, in total, was designed to support an accelerated approval pathway in the Cyclin E1-positive patient population, subject to the strength of the data, FDA feedback and the treatment landscape at the time of regulatory action.

•Part 2a: Dose confirmation evaluated two doses, 300mg QD 5:2 and 400mg QD 5:2, with approximately 30 patients enrolled per dose group. 400mg QD 5:2 was selected as the optimal monotherapy dose. Recruitment at the 300mg QD 5:2 dose level has been discontinued. All patients enrolled in Part 2a will contribute to the overall safety database submitted to the FDA.
•Part 2b: Enrollment expansion at the selected 400mg QD 5:2 dose up to approximately 100 patients, including patients at this dose in Part 2a. This cohort has completed enrollment.
•Part 2c: Broadening study population to include patients previously treated with a taxane-containing regimen for PROC. The enrollment in this cohort is scheduled to complete in November 2026.
For physician and patient information about the DENALI trial, please visit www.denalitrial.com.

About ASPENOVA Clinical Trial
ASPENOVA is a Phase 3 randomized, confirmatory clinical trial designed to support full approval of azenosertib in patients with Cyclin E1-positive PROC. The trial is expected to enroll approximately 420 patients and compare azenosertib monotherapy at 400mg QD 5:2 to investigator's choice of standard-of-care single-agent chemotherapy (paclitaxel, pegylated liposomal doxorubicin [PLD], gemcitabine, or topotecan) in this biomarker-selected population. The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS); key secondary endpoints include overall survival (OS) and overall response rate (ORR). ASPENOVA is intended to support approvals in the U.S. and major ex-U.S. markets, based on alignment with U.S. and EU regulators.
